§ 03 — Research —

Foundations,
algorithms, and
real-world impact.

Our research spans machine learning, computer vision, dynamical systems and robotics, and AI for healthcare — combining principled theory with scalable algorithms and clinical deployment.

§ 03·1

Four research areas

Tightly interlinked, often combined in single projects.

i.

Machine Learning

Deep learning theory, trustworthy AI, generative models, parsimonious representation learning, continual learning, and optimization for non-convex loss landscapes.

  • Deep theory
  • Trustworthy
  • Generative
  • Optimization
  • Continual
ii.

Computer Vision

Vision–language models, human motion understanding, video analysis, image analysis, and geometric 3D vision — with an emphasis on interpretability and generalization.

  • VLMs
  • Motion
  • 3D
  • Video
  • Interpretable
iii.

Dynamics & Robotics

Methods for analyzing and learning linear, hybrid, and multi-agent systems — from theoretical guarantees to deployment on physical platforms.

  • Hybrid
  • Multi-agent
  • Control
  • Safety
iv.

AI for Healthcare

Translating advances in AI into clinical systems for radiology, cardiology, neurology, hematology, and surgery — together with clinicians at Penn Medicine and beyond.

  • Radiology
  • Cardiology
  • Surgery
  • Neurology

Featured project

Most recent flagship work from the lab.

NeurIPS 2025
Conformal
Information
Pursuit
NeurIPS 2025 · Vidal Lab

Interactively Guiding Large Language Models, with guarantees.

We extend the information pursuit framework to LLMs with conformal calibration — letting models ask informative, targeted questions to disambiguate user intent under formal uncertainty guarantees.

Authors: Kwan Ho Ryan Chan, Yuyan Ge, Edgar Dobriban, Hamed Hassani, René Vidal.
