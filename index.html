§ 02

We work at the boundary
of theory and the world.

The VIDAL Lab develops the mathematical and algorithmic foundations of modern AI — with a sustained emphasis on trustworthy systems and real-world impact. Our research philosophy combines principled theory, scalable algorithms, and deployment in collaboration with clinicians, scientists, and engineers.

i.

Machine Learning

Deep learning theory, trustworthy AI, generative models, parsimonious representations, continual learning, and optimization.

  • Deep theory
  • Generative
  • Optimization
  • Continual
ii.

Computer Vision

Vision–language models, human motion understanding, video analysis, image analysis, and geometric 3D vision.

  • VLMs
  • Motion
  • 3D
  • Video
iii.

Dynamics & Robotics

Methods for analyzing and learning linear, hybrid and multi-agent systems — from theory to deployment on physical platforms.

  • Hybrid
  • Multi-agent
  • Control
iv.

AI for Healthcare

Translating advances in AI into clinical systems for radiology, cardiology, neurology, hematology, and surgery.

  • Radiology
  • Cardiology
  • Surgery
